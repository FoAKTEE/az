## Relevance

The seed paper for mission `ktg-train`: it defines the self-play training loop
(self-play → shuffle → train → export → gate) that the mission runs, the
auxiliary targets and architecture that KataGo's model configs implement, and the
board-size randomization machinery the mission narrows to 9×9 only. It is the
document that says *what the loop is supposed to do*; `ref-code/lightvector-KataGo`
is the artifact that says *how it is implemented today*.

## Expected role

**Seed** (primary). Supplies the method definitions and the claim skeleton for
`pipelines/1-decompose`. Not a baseline for numerical reproduction: the mission
trains a transformer trunk on 9×9, an architecture and regime the paper does not
cover (see `[OPEN] arch-gap` below).

## `[OPEN]` items

- `[OPEN] arch-gap` — The paper describes a **residual-convolutional** trunk with global
  pooling (§NNArchitecture l.356-540). It contains no transformer/attention trunk; the
  mission's `b*c*h*tf*` family postdates the paper and exists only in the code mirror
  (model version 17, `modelconfigs.py:43`). **Closes when** the decomposition stage records
  explicitly which paper claims survive the trunk substitution and which are code-only.
- `[OPEN] boardsize-gap` — The mission trains **9×9 only** (`bSizes = 9`), while the paper's
  regime, its `b_avg`/`σ²` constants (l.404) and every reported Elo (l.240, l.293) assume the
  mixed-size 9…19 distribution. Paper-reported numbers therefore do **not** transfer as
  targets. **Closes when** a 9×9-only success criterion is defined that does not cite a
  paper Elo figure.
- `[OPEN] hparam-scale` — §TrainingDetails (l.601) gives hyperparameters for a ~30-GPU,
  19-day run. The mission's budget is ≤4 GPUs. **Closes when** the scaled-down
  hyperparameters are recorded with their derivation, or measured on the cluster.
- `[OPEN] tex-compile` — the mirrored tex has not been compiled (no `latexmk` run attempted;
  `.bbl` is shipped, so a build is likely possible). Not required by this stage's V1 gate.
  **Closes when** a compile is run and its exit status recorded, if a rendered PDF is ever needed.
