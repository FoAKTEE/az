WHAT IS AND IS NOT AFFECTED
  unaffected  chain continuity -- successors ARE created, with the right
              dependency, walltime and partition set. The chain will advance.
  unaffected  the circuit breaker -- it is enforced a second way, by the
              post-trip guard at the top of the body: a successor that starts
              with failcount >= KTG_MAX_FAILS or .breaker_tripped present exits 0
              before running the loop. A crash loop still stops.
  unaffected  the STOP brake -- a successor checks $BASEDIR/STOP at entry and
              exits 0, so a storage stop still halts the chain.
  BROKEN      cancel_successor() can never fire, because SUCCESSOR is always "".
              The path that exists to cut a deterministic pre-flight failure
              early (loop.sbatch:"pre-flight failure -- the successor would
              repeat it") is dead: that successor will start, fail the same way
              and spend another breaker attempt instead of being cancelled.
  BROKEN      the storage-stop path will log "no queued successor to cancel"
              while a successor is in fact queued. Harmless (it exits 0 on STOP)
              but wrong in the record.
  MISLEADING  every link will log "chain not extended" when it did extend the
              chain, and the "successor queued:" line P9 looks for will never
              appear. The section-C boundary procedure must read the successor
              from squeue/sacct, NOT from the log.
