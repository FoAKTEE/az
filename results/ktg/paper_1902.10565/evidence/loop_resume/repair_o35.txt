queued afterany successor is not cancelled -- the o26 class, with the crash loop
then bounded only by KTG_MAX_CHAIN=200. In codes/loop/synchronous_loop_9x9.sh
the same expansion is CHANGE 10, so the loop dies after every completed cycle
without ever recording one. .failcount had no check at all: "abc" aborted
finalize with "unbound variable" (bare words are variable references inside
$(( ))), and a value past 2^63 wrapped silently.

Fix, in two files:

    chain-state integer is now read: whitespace trim, plain-decimal guard, an
    18-digit width test (the widest that cannot wrap 2^63), then `10#` to pin

    as 0 and the CALLER decides whether to log it. Applied to .failcount

  * codes/loop/synchronous_loop_9x9.sh -- the CHANGE 10 writer gets the same
